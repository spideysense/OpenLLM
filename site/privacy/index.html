Privacy at home

Your information has a home.

Last updated September 17, 2026. This page distinguishes the new household developer preview, the existing AI workspace and the public website.

Household preview

Accounts, memory and tasks are stored in an encrypted vault on the computer running Aspen. Private notes are filtered by signed-in member. Device clients receive shared household context only. Household questions go to the local Ollama runtime; the household core has no cloud-model fallback or analytics. Microphone access is disabled.

Connections you choose

Home Assistant connections send device requests to your configured local server. Downloading a model uses internet through your local model runtime. The separate online Butler service and other external services process requests under their own settings and policies. Using a cloud-hosted Aspen instance places its data on that host, rather than physically in your home.

The existing AI workspace

The separately retained AI workspace has its own optional online tools, providers and remote-access features. Its privacy behavior depends on enabled settings. Household permissions must not be assumed to apply to the legacy workspace.

This website

The public site retains its first-party visit counter and source attribution. The sample home runs example interactions in browser memory and does not access household devices. The site also links to services such as GitHub and app stores, which have their own privacy policies.

The Aspen waitlist

If you join, we store your email address, signup time, referral source and consent to receive launch and early access emails in our hosted database. This website signup is separate from the private information in your Aspen home. We do not sell waitlist addresses. The form does not send a confirmation email or verify ownership of your mailbox. Addresses remain marked as unverified; joining is not a purchase or a guaranteed place in an early access program.

You can undo a new signup from its confirmation screen. To leave later or request deletion, email hello@runonaspen.com. Any launch emails will include an unsubscribe option. We keep signup details until you request deletion or the list is no longer needed for launch and early access. To prevent abuse, a keyed hash of your IP address is kept for up to an hour; the waitlist does not store your raw IP address.

Keeping your computer secure

A local vault protects stored data, but cannot protect it from a compromised operating system. The desktop uses OS key protection where available; headless installations require careful key storage and disk encryption. This is a developer preview with further security and recovery work required before consumer release.
