## Hardware concept renderings

`aspen-hub-pod.webp` and `aspen-pod-entryway.webp` are generated photorealistic product concepts, not photographs of manufactured hardware. Created with the built-in image generation tool on September 19, 2026. PNG originals remain in the conversation's generated images; website copies are compressed WebP.

Final design brief: a consistent family of space-grey anodized aluminum flat circular discs with softly rounded edges, a larger local-compute hub and a distinctly smaller room pod. Both have a short, subtle warm-white status light and microperforated acoustic underside. The pod has a tiny rotating camera tucked into the rim. Dimensions in the generation brief were compositional guidance, not validated specifications.

Final pair prompt: Match both devices in space grey and the same flat circular disc form. Larger hub approximately 15 cm across, smaller pod approximately 8 cm across. Tiny flush camera lens, no visor or robot face. Subtle short warm-white light, no neon ring. Photograph complete pair on a walnut console in a naturally sunlit home with a blurred garden. No overlays, illustrations or additional devices.

Final scene prompt: Preserve the exact smaller space-grey disc from the pair reference. Place it beside tortoiseshell glasses and house keys on a walnut entryway table. Small unobtrusive proportions, tiny flush lens and matching subtle light. Calm wall on the left, objects in lower right, naturally lit real interior. No text, no illustrations.
